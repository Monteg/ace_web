Gold of Ra is an Egyptian-themed slot played on a classic 5×3 layout with 11 fixed paylines. The game blends traditional line wins with a progressive multiplier system that grows with each winning line and becomes even more powerful during Free Spins.

## Core Gameplay

Gold of Ra features a 5×3 reel layout with 11 fixed paylines. Winning combinations are formed when 3 or more identical symbols land from left to right on an active payline. Multiple paylines can win in a single spin, and all payouts are combined to determine the total reward.

## Main Feature

### Progressive Win Multiplier

Each winning line increases the round multiplier, boosting the total payout potential as multiple combinations appear during the same spin.

## Bonus Spins & Bonus Game

The Free Spins feature is activated when 3 or more Scatter symbols appear anywhere on the reels. The number of awarded Free Spins depends on the number of Scatter symbols that land. During the bonus round, the progressive multiplier does not reset between spins. This allows players to build increasingly powerful multipliers and unlock significantly larger win potential.

## Multiple Payline Wins

### Persistent Bonus Multiplier

During Free Spins the multiplier carries over between spins and continues to grow based on the number of winning paylines.

## Design & Atmosphere

### BUILDING A MULTIPLIER-DRIVEN EGYPTIAN SLOT

Gold of Ra combines classic slot mechanics with a dynamic multiplier system. Players win through traditional paylines across the 5×3 reel grid. Each winning line contributes to the round multiplier, increasing the value of the final payout.

During Free Spins the multiplier persists and continues to grow across spins. This structure creates a balance between classic slot simplicity and escalating win potential.
