Blackjack is a fast-paced table game where players can play up to three independent hands against one dealer.

Build each hand toward 21, use strategic actions, and beat the dealer without going bust.

## Core Gameplay

Players place one, two, or three separate bets before the deal, with each active position receiving its own two-card hand.

Each hand is played in sequence using Hit, Stand, Double, or Split when available.

After all player decisions are complete, the dealer draws according to the table rules and every hand is settled separately.

## Main Feature

Three-Hand Blackjack

Play up to three independent hands with different bet amounts against the same dealer in one round.

## Bonus Spins & Bonus Game

### ROUND FLOW

Choose chip values and place bets on the Left, Center, or Right positions before the deal.

Every active position receives two cards while the dealer shows one open card and keeps one card hidden.

Play each hand from left to right, including any additional hands created through Split.

The dealer reveals the hidden card, completes the dealer hand, and settles every player hand separately.

## Multiple Payline Wins

### EXTRA FEATURE

Independent Split System

Eligible pairs can be split into separate hands, with each active betting position supporting its own Split decisions.

## Design & Atmosphere

### BUILDING A MULTI-HAND BLACKJACK EXPERIENCE

Blackjack combines the classic goal of reaching 21 with a flexible three-position table.

Players can distribute different bet amounts across the Left, Center, and Right hands before each deal.

Every active hand follows its own sequence of decisions while playing against one shared dealer hand.

Split options can create additional hands, adding more strategic choices to the round.

Separate hand results and payouts keep every position clear from the initial deal to the final dealer comparison.
