Sweet Candy is a vibrant slot played on a dynamic 5×6 grid, combining cascading reactions with a progressive multiplier system. Wins begin from 8 matching symbols anywhere on the grid, creating rapid combinations and exciting chain reactions within a single spin.

## Core Gameplay

Sweet Candy uses a cluster-style payout system on a 5×6 grid. Wins occur when 8 or more identical symbols appear anywhere on the field, and symbols do not need to be adjacent. Winning symbols disappear and trigger cascades as new symbols fall into place, creating additional wins within the same spin while Wilds help expand combinations.

## Main Feature

### Momentum Multiplier

Sweet Candy features a progressive win multiplier that increases during cascade sequences. Each new win raises the multiplier, allowing payouts to grow rapidly as the chain reaction continues.

## Bonus Spins & Bonus Game

Landing 4 or more Scatter symbols anywhere on the grid activates the Free Spins feature. The feature awards 10 Free Spins. During Free Spins the cascading gameplay remains active. The multiplier does not reset between spins, allowing it to grow throughout the bonus round.

## Multiple Payline Wins

### Bonus Buy

Players can activate the Bonus Buy option to instantly trigger the Free Spins feature. This provides direct access to the bonus round and the game's most rewarding mechanics.

## Design & Atmosphere

### BUILDING A MOMENTUM-DRIVEN SWEET SLOT

Sweet Candy is built around fast-paced gameplay and momentum-driven rewards. The cluster payout system allows wins to form anywhere on the grid. Cascades keep the action flowing as new symbols drop after every win.

As combinations appear, the multiplier grows and boosts payouts. During Free Spins the multiplier persists, allowing players to build even greater win potential.
