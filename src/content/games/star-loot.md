Toy Grabber is an arcade-style claw game where players place a bet and launch a swinging claw to grab a toy prize.

Each round delivers a surprise toy outcome while the claw machine creates a fun skill-like gameplay illusion.

## Core Gameplay

Players choose a bet amount and risk level before starting the round.

After pressing Play, the claw drops toward the toy field and reveals a server-defined result.

The round ends with a win animation, loss animation, or prize payout based on the prize outcome.

## Main Feature

Arcade Claw Prize System

The game combines server-based prize logic with a visual claw machine experience.

## Bonus Spins & Bonus Game

### RESULT STATES

Winning rounds show the claw grabbing a toy and dropping it into the prize tray.

Losing rounds can appear as a miss, stuck toy, or dropped prize animation.

Small and medium wins display a short reward animation with the prize and multiplier.

Big Win and Jackpot results trigger enhanced celebration screens and stronger visual effects.

## Multiple Payline Wins

### EXTRA FEATURE

Risk Level Selection

Players can choose risk levels that adjust volatility, win frequency, and prize potential.

## Design & Atmosphere

### BUILDING AN ARCADE CLAW EXPERIENCE

Toy Grabber transforms slot-style gameplay into a playful claw machine format.

The swinging claw creates a strong timing illusion while the server controls the final result.

Toy prizes are grouped into different payout tiers, from low-value rewards to jackpot items.

Risk levels let players choose between steadier smaller wins and higher-potential outcomes.

This creates a light, visual, and replayable game loop built around anticipation and prize reveals.
