Toy Story is a crash-style claw game where players grab toys step by step while building a rising payout multiplier.

After every successful grab, collect the current reward with Cash Out or risk another attempt for a higher return.

## Core Gameplay

Players choose a bet amount and Low, Medium, or High risk before starting the round.

Each grab is a new step: success raises the multiplier and potential payout, while failure ends the round and loses the bet.

The final payout is awarded only when the player uses Cash Out during an active round.

## Main Feature

Grabber Crash Progression

Each successful toy grab advances the round and raises the multiplier, creating a new risk-or-collect decision after every step.

## Bonus Spins & Bonus Game

### ROUND FLOW

Set the bet and risk level, then launch the claw for the first grab.

A successful grab updates the multiplier and unlocks the Cash Out option.

Continue the round to attempt another toy or collect the current potential payout.

A failed grab ends the round with no payout, while Cash Out secures the accumulated reward.

## Multiple Payline Wins

### EXTRA FEATURE

Three Risk Levels

Low, Medium, and High modes adjust successful grab frequency and multiplier growth.

## Design & Atmosphere

### BUILDING A GRABBER CRASH EXPERIENCE

Toy Story transforms step-multiplier gameplay into a playful claw machine experience.

The swinging claw adds an arcade timing feel as players target toys across the prize field.

Every successful grab raises the current multiplier and keeps the round active.

Low, Medium, and High risk modes balance grab frequency against faster reward growth.

Cash Out lets players secure accumulated value before a failed attempt ends the round.
